An active mint authority can create additional tokens. This is a risk indicator, not proof of wrongdoing.

Freeze authority

A freeze authority can restrict transfers from token accounts.

Holder concentration

A few accounts may hold much of the supply. Accounts do not necessarily represent different people.

Liquidity evidence

Liquidity data describes available pool assets. Lock information may be incomplete or unverified.
