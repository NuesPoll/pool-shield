Privacy

Pool Shield has no accounts, no wallet connection and no sign-up. This page says plainly what it does collect.

What is collected

  • The token addresses that are scanned. They appear in the site's server logs, with a score and verdict, so I can count usage. They are not linked to your name.
  • Anonymous visit counts from Vercel Web Analytics.
  • An optional group tag. If a link ends in ?src=something, that tag is logged so I can see which group a visit came from.

What stays on your device

  • Your language choice and your recent scans are saved in your own browser only. The Clear button removes the scans.
  • QR photos you choose are read in your browser and are not uploaded.
  • The camera is used only when you tap Scan QR, and only to read a QR code.

Outside services that see a token address

To work, Pool Shield sends the token address to a Solana node provider, to Dexscreener, and to RugCheck. They do not receive your name or device details from Pool Shield. Their own policies apply to what they log.

The feedback form

The feedback form runs on Google Forms. It asks for a first name or nickname, which is optional, and Google's policy applies. Answers are quoted in reports only with permission, and only by first name.

Questions or removal

To ask a question or request removal of feedback you submitted, open an issue. Pool Shield is an independent student project and is not financial advice.
